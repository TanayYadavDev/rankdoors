## What CollegeDoors is (verified from public pages, Oct 2026)

- A **B2B-first, white-label** academic infrastructure platform owned by
  **Seshat Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (Mumbai, started 2015)**, sold to
  coaching institutes, schools, junior colleges, and individual teachers —
  not primarily to students directly. Institutes get their own branded
  portal ("your logo, your branding", 10-day full-access trial, "no
  technical setup required"). A smaller B2C shop (`learn.collegedoors.com`)
  sells test-series packages directly to students.
- Three paper-creation modes: **Standard** (fixed exam format),
  **TIPS** (teacher's manual/custom paper setting: choose subject →
  section → chapter → topic → difficulty → pattern, 10–40 questions at a
  time, own bank or CollegeDoors bank, review, schedule), and
  **ATM / Automated Test Maker** (system-generated paper from minimum
  inputs).
- Exams named: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET, BITSAT, MHT-CET, NTSE.
- Reports: **PAR** (individual diagnostics: time distribution,
  question-level accuracy, recommendations), **BPR** (batch heatmap),
  **CTR** (cumulative across tests), percentile & insights. Behaviour
  tracking: per-question time, revisits, preview/review/skip, a "Guess"
  button. A paid add-on, **TRACE**, offers AI-written descriptive
  feedback (Aug 2025 release notes). No AI question-generation or AI
  doubt-solver was found.
- Test player features evidenced in release notes/FAQ: question palette
  with Marked-for-Review states, section switching, passcode for
  scheduled tests, custom instructions, dark mode/accessibility per NTA
  guidelines, cheating-prevention options, admin Force Submit, result
  hold/delay, 20-minute reconnect buffer (answers saved), minimum
  512 Kbps, internet always required (no true offline mode).
- Printable papers: question paper + solutions + answer key as PDF/Excel,
  institute logo/watermark, randomised Sets A–D, password protection.
  Offline flow evidenced is **Excel response upload** (100+ students),
  not camera OMR scanning.
- Question bank: described only qualitatively ("large", "comprehensive").
  Tagging: Subject → Section → Chapter → Topic, difficulty, pattern/
  subtype, past-year flag, used/unused, source, upload date. Teachers can
  upload their own questions (single Question Designer + bulk PDF
  extraction). An "error-free" claim and a Report-Error button exist.
- Study material: digital e-books, printed branded modules, digital
  library (videos/notes). Course Content rules: teachers upload PDFs
  directly; videos only as YouTube links (public/unlisted); students
  cannot download course PDFs/videos. Live classes: **Zoom integration**,
  scheduled only by an Admin, 100-participant base-plan limit, recordings
  via the institute's own YouTube channel — CollegeDoors hosts no video.
- Roles: Student, Teacher, Parent (mapped by the student; CTR access;
  can view activity but cannot reopen a test; paid SMS alerts), Institute
  Admin (bulk users, batches, scheduling, notifications, Force Submit),
  Channel Partner (reseller class named in Terms).
- Products: main white-label platform; **Foundation**
  (`foundation.collegedoors.com`) for Classes 8–10 teachers — downloadable
  personalised question-paper packages, unlimited validity; B2C test
  series store.
- Mobile app: free app (web + app test-taking, reports, live-class
  notifications). Languages listed: English. No public evidence of a
  Hindi/regional-language UI.
- Business model: B2B SaaS + content licensing, plus B2C packages and
  paid add-ons (SMS, TRACE AI reporting). **No current B2B price list
  was found publicly.** The only rupee figures found are historic
  (2017): student subscription "₹340–2,500" — **never quote as current.**

## Differentiation opportunities (recommendations, not facts)

Within-batch percentile only (an All-India percentile needs pooled
multi-institute data). Honest behaviour facts ("spent 6 min on Q12,
changed answer 3×") instead of unvalidated trait claims ("anxiety").
Single-school strengths: per-answer autosave (beats the original's
20-minute buffer), one unified site, Hindi/Hinglish UI option later,
WhatsApp parent reports in Phase 2, PWA before native app.
