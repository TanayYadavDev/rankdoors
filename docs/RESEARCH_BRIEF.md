## What CollegeDoors is (verified from public pages, Oct 2026)

- **Platform & Company:** A B2B-first, white-label academic testing and content infrastructure platform owned and operated by **Seshat Technologies Pvt. Ltd.** (incorporated 2015; registered office: 282, Powai Plaza, Hiranandani Gardens, Powai, Mumbai 400076). Targeted at coaching institutes, schools, junior colleges, and independent educators ("your logo, your branding", 10-day full-access trial, "no technical setup required"). Direct-to-student retail occurs via `learn.collegedoors.com`.
  - *Sources:* [collegedoors.com](https://www.collegedoors.com/), [About Us](https://www.collegedoors.com/pages/about.html), [Terms of Use](https://www.collegedoors.com/pages/terms.html).

- **Paper Creation Modes:**
  1. **Standard:** Pre-configured fixed exam formats.
  2. **TIPS (Teacher's Interface for Paper Setting):** Custom paper creation allowing teachers to filter by Subject → Section → Chapter → Topic → Difficulty → Question Pattern, drawing 10–40 questions from either CollegeDoors' question bank or the teacher's own uploaded bank, reviewing, setting duration, and scheduling.
  3. **ATM (Automated Test Maker):** Algorithmic test generation from syllabus and difficulty constraints (e.g., full syllabus class/chapter distribution).
  - *Sources:* [collegedoors.com](https://www.collegedoors.com/), [Release Notes](https://www.collegedoors.com/releasenotes).

- **Target Exams:** IIT-JEE (Main & Advanced), NEET, MHT-CET, BITSAT, and NTSE.
  - *Source:* [collegedoors.com FAQ](https://www.collegedoors.com/).

- **Reporting & Analytics:**
  - **PAR (Performance Analysis Report):** Individual test diagnostics covering score, subject breakdown, time distribution, question-level accuracy, and behavioral metrics (e.g., target time, revisits).
  - **BPR (Batch Performance Report):** Multi-student comparative heatmaps identifying collective weak areas across chapters/topics.
  - **CTR (Cumulative Test Report):** Aggregated multi-test historical report tracking progression, tests taken/missed ("Absent"), ranks, and batch highest scores.
  - **TRACE (Paid Add-On):** Introduced in August 2025 release notes as "TRACE Enhanced AI-based Reporting", providing AI-generated descriptive diagnostic feedback. No AI question-generation or AI doubt-solver exists.
  - *Sources:* [collegedoors.com](https://www.collegedoors.com/), [Release Notes](https://www.collegedoors.com/releasenotes).

- **Test Player Features:**
  - NTA-pattern interface: timer, question palette with Marked-for-Review status, question-number navigation panel, section switching.
  - Passcode protection for scheduled tests, custom instructions, dark mode per accessibility guidelines, screen magnifier tool, right-panel hover tooltips.
  - Numerical value questions with on-screen keypad.
  - Proctoring & Cheating Prevention: fullscreen locks, tab switch detection.
  - Connectivity & Submission: 20-minute reconnect buffer saving responses, auto-submit on timer expiry, explicit "Submitted by Institute Admin" tag for force-submits, result hold/delay options.
  - *Sources:* [collegedoors.com](https://www.collegedoors.com/), [Release Notes (Dec 2025 – Sep 2026)](https://www.collegedoors.com/releasenotes).

- **Printable Papers & Offline Testing:**
  - Generation of custom question papers, answer keys, and solutions in PDF/Excel format.
  - Features institute logo/watermark, single-column or double-column layouts with vertical dividers, header logo size sliders, optional current date and test duration/marks on headers, randomised question sets (Sets A–D), password protection.
  - Offline response capture supported via Excel bulk response upload (optimized for 100+ students); no camera-based OMR scanning.
  - *Sources:* [collegedoors.com](https://www.collegedoors.com/), [Release Notes (Feb – Sep 2026)](https://www.collegedoors.com/releasenotes).

- **Question Bank & Authoring:**
  - Hierarchical tagging: Subject → Section → Chapter → Topic, difficulty tier, pattern/subtype, past-year question (PYQ) flags, used/unused status.
  - Ingestion: Single "Question Designer" tool + bulk extraction via "Question Extractor" (QE) from PDFs.
  - Error reporting workflow via an in-test/in-report "Report Error" button reviewed by subject faculty.
  - *Sources:* [collegedoors.com](https://www.collegedoors.com/), [Release Notes](https://www.collegedoors.com/releasenotes).

- **Study Material & Live Classes:**
  - Digital library for PDFs and study notes; video lectures integrated strictly as embedded/unlisted YouTube links (no video hosting on CollegeDoors infrastructure). Students cannot download course PDFs/videos directly.
  - Live classes conducted via Zoom integration scheduled by Institute Admin (100-participant base tier limit).
  - *Sources:* [collegedoors.com Terms](https://www.collegedoors.com/pages/terms.html), [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.collegedoors.student).

- **Roles & User Management:**
  - **Student:** test taking, PAR/CTR reports, question bookmarking, announcements.
  - **Teacher:** paper authoring (TIPS/ATM), scheduling, question bank management, PAR/BPR/CTR inspection.
  - **Parent:** read-only view of child's CTR report and test activity (mapped by student account, cannot reopen tests).
  - **Institute Admin:** bulk user/batch creation, package mapping, force submit, announcement broadcasting, live class scheduling.
  - *Sources:* [collegedoors.com](https://www.collegedoors.com/), [Terms of Use](https://www.collegedoors.com/pages/terms.html).

- **Subdomains & Mobile App:**
  - **Foundation (`foundation.collegedoors.com`):** Tailored specifically for Classes 8, 9, and 10 teachers and schools, offering downloadable personalized question-paper packages with customizable difficulty levels and unlimited validity.
  - **Mobile Companion App:** Android app on Google Play ("CollegeDoors" by Seshat Technologies) for test taking, report access, and live class notifications. English interface only.
  - *Sources:* [foundation.collegedoors.com](https://foundation.collegedoors.com/), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.collegedoors.student).

- **Pricing & Commercial Terms:**
  - No public rupee pricing sheet is published on `collegedoors.com/pages/pricing.html`.
  - Offers a **10-day Free Trial** with full white-label access (custom logo, watermark, test creation, analytics).
  - Commercial contracts are customized B2B SaaS quotes based on student count and exam streams, offering packages with infinite validity.
  - Historic 2017 direct-to-student subscription figures (₹340–2,500) are obsolete and must **never** be cited as current pricing.
  - *Sources:* [Pricing Page](https://www.collegedoors.com/pages/pricing.html), [Terms of Use](https://www.collegedoors.com/pages/terms.html).

## Differentiation opportunities (recommendations, not facts)

1. **Batch-Honest Percentile:** RankDoors computes percentile and rank **strictly within the SPS Bhopal student cohort**, avoiding misleading pseudo-"All-India" claims.
2. **Deterministic Autosave:** Instant local/cloud response saving per question ("Save & Next" + immediate state persistence), superior to CollegeDoors' 20-minute reconnect buffer window.
3. **Single Unified Web App:** Eliminate CollegeDoors' fragmented user experience (which redirects logged-in students between an outer portal and an older legacy test engine).
4. **Clean Modern Design System:** Dedicated Sagar Public School matte wine-maroon palette (`#722F37` / `#451E24`) with matched light/dark modes and zero eye strain, replacing generic legacy white-label styling.
5. **Phase 2 Expansion:** Automated WhatsApp report delivery directly to parents and PWA mobile-first offline caching.
